Alpha Centauri: The Three-Star System

Two stars circle each other every 79.76 years. Proxima Centauri circles the pair over about 550,000 years.

Data: A–B period 79.76 years, eccentricity 0.519, semi-major axis 23.3 AU (closest 11.2 AU, farthest 35.6 AU). Proxima period about 550,000 years, eccentricity about 0.50, closest 4,300 AU, farthest 13,000 AU. Assumed here: masses of 1.1 and 0.9 solar masses, A–B pair seen face-on (the real orbit is tilted 79.2° to our view), and the Proxima orbit drawn flat. Sizes of the stars are not to scale, and the two panels run on different clocks. Speeds come from the vis-viva equation.
